Roadmap

Domain resources ship as milestone M9 of the orchestration roadmap, in slices that each deliver something usable. The first slice needs no orchestration at all. This page is where the milestone is tracked. The design holds the contract and its open decisions, and issue #173 holds the discussion.

Done when: Colocated team declarations feed independently registered units. Local and CI runs produce identical bundles and execution keys. Moving a declaration without changing its membership or admission re-runs nothing. Schema, policy, and missing-evidence failures block only the consuming units, before any effect. Releases select their manifests and are selected by label or path, read the same bundles in every render, and render identically with and without the cache. The guide runs end to end.
