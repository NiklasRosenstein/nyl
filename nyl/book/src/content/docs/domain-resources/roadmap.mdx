Domain resources ship as milestone M9 of the
[orchestration roadmap](/nyl/orchestration/roadmap/#m9),
in slices that each deliver something usable. The first slice needs no
orchestration at all. This page is where the milestone is tracked. The
[design](https://github.com/NiklasRosenstein/nyl/blob/develop/design/domain-resources.md)
holds the contract and its open decisions, and
[issue #173](https://github.com/NiklasRosenstein/nyl/issues/173) holds the
discussion.

when all of it has. The line between slices fills as the slice above it
